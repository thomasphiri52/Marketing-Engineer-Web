FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Marketing Engineering?

It combines marketing strategy, data, analytics and technology to build measurable systems for better marketing decisions.

Who are the packages for?

The programmes are designed for organisations that need marketing intelligence, analytics, dashboards, automation and growth support.

Why is the programme 12 months?

A 12-month engagement allows time for discovery, implementation, measurement, optimisation and strategic review.

Can packages be customised?

Yes. Final scope and deliverables are agreed in the proposal and statement of work.

Do you build dashboards?

Yes. Dashboard and business intelligence solutions can be designed around available data and decision-making requirements.

Can existing business data be integrated?

Yes, subject to available systems, permissions, APIs and data quality.

How are reports delivered?

Reporting can be provided through dashboards, scheduled reports and executive summaries according to the agreed programme.

How does onboarding work?

After agreement, the programme starts with discovery, objectives, data requirements, access and the first delivery plan.
