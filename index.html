COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear expectations from the start.

Are the ROI numbers guaranteed?

No. The calculator is an illustrative model based on the inputs entered. Real outcomes depend on customer behaviour, costs, lead quality, market conditions and execution.

Are all portfolio examples completed client deployments?

Not necessarily. The site labels concepts and prototypes as such. A live demo link shows an accessible prototype; it does not imply a particular business outcome or endorsement.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Potentially. The proposal process should establish your current tools, data access, security requirements and integration scope before a commitment is made.

What happens after I submit a brief?

The page will create a project summary and attempt to send the enquiry to the configured backend. If the backend is not configured, it will offer an email fallback. Submission is not confirmed until you see a success message or email draft.
